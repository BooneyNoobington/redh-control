# Privacy policy: Redh Control

As of November 2026.

Redh Control collects no data. It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and nothing
goes to its developer or to anyone else.

The app talks only to what you set up in it: the RedhAPI of your servers, and your FRITZ!Boxes.
It talks to them directly from your device, over connections whose certificates it verifies.

What you enter stays on your devices: the setup in the app's own storage, tokens and passwords in
the Keychain. The iPhone app passes them to Redh Control on your Apple Watch, through Apple's
WatchConnectivity, and nowhere else. The Mac app keeps a setup of its own.

Questions go to the address on the support page.
